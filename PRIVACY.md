# Privacy & Data Management

## Contact Information

For any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your survey data:

**Email**: survey-x7qp@adobe.com

## What to Contact Us About

### Data Removal Requests
If you've participated in the survey and want your data removed:
1. Email survey-x7qp@adobe.com with "Data Removal Request" in the subject
2. Include the email address you used in the survey
3. We will remove all associated data within 7 business days

### Technical Issues
Report problems such as:
- Survey not loading properly
- Images not displaying
- Form submission errors
- Browser compatibility issues

### Study Questions
Ask about:
- Purpose of the study
- How data will be used
- Publication plans
- Results availability

## Data We Collect

### Information You Provide
- Email address
- Professional background (occupation, education level)
- AI/graphics experience
- Image quality evaluations
- Prompt adherence assessments
- Confidence ratings

### Automatically Collected
- Browser name and version
- Operating system
- Screen resolution
- Pixel density ratio
- Color depth
- Viewport dimensions
- Timestamp of participation

### What We Don't Collect
- IP addresses (not stored)
- Precise location data
- Personal browsing history
- Third-party cookies
- Analytics beyond basic survey completion

## How We Use Your Data

**Research Purposes Only:**
- Analyze image quality preferences
- Understand how different displays affect perception
- Study correlation between experience and evaluation
- Improve AI image generation systems

**Not Used For:**
- Marketing or advertising
- Selling to third parties
- User profiling outside this study
- Any commercial purposes

## Data Storage & Security

- Stored in SQLite database on secure server
- Database backups encrypted
- Access limited to authorized researchers
- No data sharing with external parties without consent

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
1. **Access**: Request a copy of your data
2. **Rectification**: Correct any inaccurate information
3. **Erasure**: Request deletion of your data
4. **Portability**: Receive your data in a structured format
5. **Withdraw**: Stop participating at any time (partial data may be retained)

## Data Retention

- Survey responses: Retained until study completion + publication period
- After study conclusion: Data may be anonymized and archived for academic purposes
- Upon request: Individual data can be removed at any time

## GDPR Compliance

If you are in the EU/EEA:
- Lawful basis: Consent and legitimate scientific interest
- Data controller: Adobe Research (survey administrator)
- Right to lodge complaint with supervisory authority

## CCPA Compliance

If you are a California resident:
- Right to know what data is collected
- Right to deletion
- Right to opt-out of sale (not applicable - we don't sell data)
- No discrimination for exercising rights

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be posted at this location with an updated date.

## Study Completion

When the study concludes:
1. Raw emails will be removed from active dataset
2. Data will be pseudonymized for analysis
3. Results may be published in academic papers (aggregate only)
4. Participants can request notification when results are available
