Privacy Policy & Data Collection

Last Updated: November 12, 2025

Important Notice: This privacy policy describes our current data handling practices. We recommend consulting with your own legal counsel if you have specific privacy concerns. By participating in this survey, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

Overview

This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we intend to use it, and information about your rights regarding your personal information. We strive to protect your privacy and be transparent about our data collection practices.

What Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Email Address: Used to identify your responses and prevent duplicate submissions
  • Occupation/Role: Helps us understand participant backgrounds
  • Technical Background: Your level of technical expertise
  • AI Experience: Your experience with AI image generation systems and tools
  • Work Information: Whether you work with AI systems or computer graphics
  • AI Familiarity: Your overall familiarity with AI/ML concepts
  • Survey Responses: Your image evaluations, ratings, and confidence levels

Information We Automatically Collect

To understand how images appear on different devices, we collect the following technical information:

  • Browser Type and Version: (e.g., Chrome 120.0)
  • Operating System: (e.g., macOS, Windows, Linux)
  • Screen Resolution: (e.g., 1920 × 1080)
  • Device Pixel Ratio: (e.g., 2x for Retina displays)
  • Color Depth: (e.g., 24-bit)
  • Viewport Size: Your browser window dimensions
  • Session Information: Anonymous session ID (cookie-based)

Note: This device information is displayed to you before submission so you know exactly what is being collected.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for research purposes:

  • Analyzing image evaluation patterns and preferences
  • Understanding how technical background affects image assessment
  • Studying how device characteristics impact image perception
  • Improving AI image generation systems
  • Academic research and publication (in aggregate, anonymized form)

Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Storage: Data is stored in a database with access controls
  • Access: We limit access to authorized personnel
  • Encryption: Data transmission uses HTTPS encryption where available
  • Retention: We intend to retain data for research purposes; exact retention periods may vary
  • Anonymization: We intend to anonymize or pseudonymize data before publication

Please note: While we implement reasonable security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Your Rights

We aim to honor the following rights regarding your personal data, subject to applicable law and legitimate research interests:

Right to Access

You may request information about the data we have collected about you.

Right to Rectification

You may request correction of inaccurate data where feasible.

Right to Erasure

You may request deletion of your data. We will make reasonable efforts to comply, though some data may need to be retained for research integrity or legal obligations.

Right to Data Portability

You may request your data in a commonly used format where technically feasible.

Right to Withdraw

You may withdraw from the survey at any time. Note that data already collected may be retained for research purposes unless you specifically request deletion.

Note: These rights may be subject to limitations based on applicable law, research integrity requirements, and technical constraints.

Cookies and Tracking

We use session cookies for technical purposes including:

  • Maintaining your survey progress
  • Preventing duplicate submissions
  • Tracking completion status

These cookies are necessary for the survey to function. We do not intend to use third-party tracking or analytics services beyond what is required for basic operation.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Third-party hosting or database services that help operate this survey
  • Research Purposes: Aggregate, anonymized findings may be published in academic papers or shared with research collaborators
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Institutional Requirements: With our institution's research oversight bodies if required

We intend to anonymize or pseudonymize any shared research data to minimize personal information exposure.

Children's Privacy

This survey is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us so we can address the situation.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued participation after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
