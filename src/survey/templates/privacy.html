Privacy Policy & Data Collection

Last Updated: November 12, 2025

Overview

This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data collection practices.

What Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Email Address: Used to identify your responses and prevent duplicate submissions
  • Occupation/Role: Helps us understand participant backgrounds
  • Technical Background: Your level of technical expertise
  • AI Experience: Your experience with AI image generation systems and tools
  • Work Information: Whether you work with AI systems or computer graphics
  • AI Familiarity: Your overall familiarity with AI/ML concepts
  • Survey Responses: Your image evaluations, ratings, and confidence levels

Information We Automatically Collect

To understand how images appear on different devices, we collect the following technical information:

  • Browser Type and Version: (e.g., Chrome 120.0)
  • Operating System: (e.g., macOS, Windows, Linux)
  • Screen Resolution: (e.g., 1920 × 1080)
  • Device Pixel Ratio: (e.g., 2x for Retina displays)
  • Color Depth: (e.g., 24-bit)
  • Viewport Size: Your browser window dimensions
  • Session Information: Anonymous session ID (cookie-based)

Note: This device information is displayed to you before submission so you know exactly what is being collected.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for research purposes:

  • Analyzing image evaluation patterns and preferences
  • Understanding how technical background affects image assessment
  • Studying how device characteristics impact image perception
  • Improving AI image generation systems
  • Academic research and publication (in aggregate, anonymized form)

Data Storage and Security

  • Storage: All data is stored in a secure database
  • Access: Only authorized researchers have access to the data
  • Encryption: Data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS
  • Retention: Data is retained for the duration of the research study
  • Anonymization: Published results will not include personally identifiable information

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

You can request a copy of all data we have collected about you.

Right to Rectification

You can request correction of any inaccurate data.

Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)

You can request deletion of your data at any time.

Right to Data Portability

You can request your data in a machine-readable format.

Right to Withdraw Consent

You can withdraw your consent and request data deletion at any time without penalty.

Cookies and Tracking

We use session cookies to:

  • Maintain your survey progress
  • Prevent duplicate submissions
  • Track survey completion status

These are essential cookies required for the survey to function. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Aggregate, anonymized research findings may be:

  • Published in academic papers or conference proceedings
  • Shared with research collaborators
  • Used in presentations or reports

Any shared data will be fully anonymized and will not contain personally identifiable information.

Children's Privacy

This survey is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued participation after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
